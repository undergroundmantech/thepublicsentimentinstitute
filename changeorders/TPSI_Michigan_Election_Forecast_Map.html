Read before quoting a county

County margins are not findings. Geography barely replicates across our two survey modes (r = +0.15, against +0.98 for age), so region was shrunk to 15% weight. County variation is demographic composition, not local political texture.

Turnout figures are a model, not a count. The 2024 baseline is real; the registration and excitement adjustments are estimates.
