# Katie Hobbs research notes

Compiled October 23, 2026. Every URL below was fetched and its body read unless it is listed under Gaps. The research ran out of web search budget partway through. Later threads used only URLs already found, plus Ballotpedia and the House Clerk.

## Thread 1: Committees and money in

### Takeaway
Elect Katie Hobbs raised about $9.5 million to $9.8 million in the cycle through June 30, 2026. The campaign says most gifts are under $100. An aligned PAC, Copper State Values, raised $2.7 million or more in 2025 from donors no article named.

- Ballotpedia: $9.8M raised, $6M spent as of June 30, 2026. https://ballotpedia.org/Arizona_gubernatorial_election,_2026
- KTAR, July 17, 2026: $9.5M full cycle; $7.4M spent and $1.7M raised in spring; about $1.4M cash heading into the July 21 primary. https://ktar.com/arizona-politics-news/election/katie-hobbs-cash-andy-biggs/5889960/
- Arizona Mirror, July 16, 2026: Q2 2026 start cash over $7.1M, raised about $2.6M, spent $7.4M, end cash nearly $2.4M. https://azmirror.com/?p=27721
- Fox News, July 24, 2026: same Q2 figures; Nicole DeMont response quote. https://foxnews.com/politics/battleground-dem-governor-torched-drunken-sailor-spending-gop-critic-says-symbolic-bigger-problem
- Campaign release, Oct 15, 2025: $12M toward 2026, of which $9.3M+ went to the campaign since 2022 and $2.7M+ to Copper State Values; nearly 95 percent of gifts under $100; paid for by Elect Katie Hobbs. https://katiehobbs.org/arizonans-rally-behind-katie-hobbs-with-record-breaking-12-million-raised-for-2026-election/
- AZ Capitol Times, Oct 16, 2025: over $4M raised in 2025; $5.4M cash; Copper State Values over $2.7M. https://azcapitoltimes.com/news/2025/10/16/gubernatorial-candidates-rake-in-millions-in-donations-ahead-of-2026
