- **Main message.** Get Nevada "out of this Lombardo Trump economy": lower costs, limits on corporate home buying, a drug price cap, data centers that pay for their own power, and a governor who is not bought by billionaire donors. https://www.kolotv.com/2026/10/09/ford-debates-an-empty-podium-alleges-republicans-tried-cancel-candidate-forum-altogether/ and https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/10/13/lombardos-policies-designed-to-benefit-campaign-contributors-not-nevadans-ford-says/

- **Strongest coalition.** Hispanic voters, 51 to 20, and women, 48 to 37, in Emerson's September poll, plus organized labor led by the Culinary Union's door knocking. https://emersoncollegepolling.com/nevada-2026-poll-ford-and-lombardo/ and https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/10/22/kamala-harris-rallies-with-aaron-ford-culinary-union-ahead-nevada-election/

- **Recent event.** Kamala Harris rallied for him at the Culinary Union in Las Vegas on October 22, 2026, calling him a chance to elect Nevada's first Black governor. https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/10/22/kamala-harris-rallies-with-aaron-ford-culinary-union-ahead-nevada-election/
- **Money gap.** He trails: $1.3 million campaign cash to Lombardo's $2.8 million, nearly $2.7 million in PAC money to more than $5.8 million excluding governors groups, and $14.1 million in DGA side ads to $29.3 million for Better Nevada PAC, though his campaign outraised Lombardo's directly in the third quarter. https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/our-top-takeaways-from-nevadas-new-campaign-finance-reports
