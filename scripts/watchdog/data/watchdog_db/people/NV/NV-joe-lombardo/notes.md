## Campaign intel

- **Main message.** A record of results and restraint: education, health care, public safety and the economy, a no new taxes pledge and a record veto pen against the Democratic Legislature, paired with attacks on Ford's out of state travel. https://ballotpedia.org/Joe_Lombardo and https://thenevadaindependent.com/elections/2026/general/key
- **Biggest vulnerability as opponents frame it.** The "Lombardo Trump economy": high fuel, housing and health costs, his tariff remark that people should "feel a little pain," vetoes of drug cost and renter bills, and policies Ford says serve billionaire donors like Bigelow. https://democraticgovernors.org/updates/a-more-affordable-nevada-releases-new-ad-slamming-lombardo-trump-economy-that-puts-billionaires-first/ and https://nevadacurrent.com/2026/10/13/lombardos-policies-designed-to-benefit-campaign-contributors-not-nevadans-ford-says/
- **Strongest coalition.** White voters, 51 to 38, and men, 47 to 41, in Emerson's September poll, with a 49 to 39 favorable rating; voters trust him over Ford on public safety and crime by 12 points in Noble Predictive Insights. https://emersoncollegepolling.com/nevada-2026-poll-ford-and-lombardo/ and https://www.noblepredictiveinsights.com/post/lombardo-tries-to-escape-trump-s-orbit-up-2-over-ford
- **Weak spot in polling.** Hispanic voters, where he trails 20 to 51, and Trump's 37 to 55 approval among likely voters; his economic approval is 45 to 47 in Noble, and RedBox Research had him down 43 to 46 on October 23. https://emersoncollegepolling.com/nevada-2026-poll-ford-and-lombardo/ and https://www.pollsmax.com/governor/nevada/
- **Recent event.** At an October fundraiser he said "There's no place easier to vote in the nation than Nevada. Unfortunately," reported by the Nevada Current on October 22 and pushed by the state Democratic Party; his campaign did not comment. Earlier he skipped the October 9 Carson City forum, leaving Ford at an empty podium. https://www.einpresswire.com/article/951683384/as-nevadans-head-to-the-polls-lombardo-laments-there-s-no-place-easier-to-vote-unfortunately and https://www.kolotv.com/2026/10/09/ford-debates-an-empty-podium-alleges-republicans-tried-cancel-candidate-forum-altogether/
- **Money gap.** He leads: $2.8 million campaign cash to Ford's $1.3 million, more than $5.8 million in PAC money to Ford's $2.7 million excluding governors groups, and $29.3 million in Better Nevada PAC ads to $14.1 million for the DGA side, though Ford outraised his campaign directly in the third quarter. https://thenevadaindependent.com/article/our-top-takeaways-from-nevadas-new-campaign-finance-reports
