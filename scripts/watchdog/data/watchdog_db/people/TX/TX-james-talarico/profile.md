# James Talarico Watchdog File

**Subject:** James Talarico, Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate in Texas, 2026, a state representative and former teacher
**Compiled:** October 25, 2026 for the On Point Politics watchdog database, onpointpolitics.us
**Data cutoff:** September 30, 2026 for campaign money, as reported October 15 to 21, 2026. Outside spending through mid October 2026.
**Next scheduled data drop:** FEC pre-general report covering through October 14, 2026, and the post-general report due December 3, 2026.

## Bottom line up front

Talarico is the best funded Senate challenger in the country and the most outspent. His campaign says it raised $64 million from July through September 2026, a record for a Senate third quarter, and more than $134 million since launch, per the Texas Tribune on October 15, 2026, https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/15/james-talarico-64-million-fundraising-texas-senate/. It spent $67.2 million in the quarter and held $14.6 million on September 30. The campaign says 97 percent of third quarter gifts were under $100.

Against him, Elon Musk put $158 million into the Senate Leadership Fund, which moved the same amounts into a new committee called Texas PAC, per the Texas Tribune on October 15, 2026, https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/15/elon-musk-ken-paxton-donations-senate-gop-super-pac/. Outside groups led by SLF had put more than $217 million into ads by the end of September.

Three interests combine money and a live decision:

1. **Small donors.** His campaign money is broad and small. The lever is his own message: ending billionaire control of politics. That is a promise he would be measured against on campaign finance votes.
2. **Large Democratic donors through Lone Star Rising PAC.** The Katy News, citing FEC filings, lists Reid Hoffman at $10 million and Government That Works PAC at $3.75 million among the backers of a pro-Talarico outside group. These are the only large checks found on his side.
3. **Musk and Senate Republican leadership, on the other side.** Their money is aimed at defeating him. The decision is control of a 53 to 47 Senate in January 2027.

## Committee structure and treasurer

The principal campaign committee's FEC ID and treasurer were not verified in this pass, because fec.gov is blocked for this project. Campaign manager Seth Krasne speaks for the campaign. The Katy News on October 21, 2026 names Lone Star Rising PAC as an outside group backing Talarico, with $31.27 million in total receipts through September 30, 2026, https://thekatynews.com/2026/10/21/james-talarico-ken-paxton-2026-campaign-donors/. That outlet relays the FEC summary for his campaign as $128,772,835 in receipts and $65,898,722 in unitemized individual gifts. The campaign's own figure is over $134 million. The two are stored separately and not reconciled.

## Small donors

Documented. The $64 million third quarter is more than triple Paxton's $18 million in the same period. It also beats Colin Allred's $30 million in 2024 and Beto O'Rourke's $38 million in 2018 for the same quarter, per the Texas Tribune. The campaign said grassroots contributors are coming together "to end billionaire control over our politics." Krasne said billionaire backed groups were spending over $200 million "to lie about James."

Cash fell over the summer. Talarico held $21.5 million in late July against Paxton's $7.5 million, per AP reporting carried by NBC DFW on July 27, 2026, https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/politics/lone-star-politics/years-of-scandals-havent-stopped-ken-paxton-can-james-talarico-change-that/4054656/. By September 30 he held $14.6 million.

Inference: A base this wide gives no single donor leverage over him. The risk to watch is the reverse. His message commits him to campaign finance and anticorruption votes, and he will be judged on whether he keeps it once he needs party committees to defend the seat.

## Large Democratic donors

Documented, all from The Katy News relaying FEC filings, so all Secondary. Lone Star Rising PAC received $10 million from Reid Hoffman by the June 30 filing, with $11.5 million cumulative. Government That Works PAC gave $1 million on January 22 and $2.75 million on January 28, 2026. America Votes gave $900,000, Christian Larsen $500,000 and Amos B. Hostetter Jr. $250,000, all from January to June 2026. Susan Sarofim, Don Henley and Gus Dwairy each gave $100,000. John Stryker gave $35,000 and Eric Schwartz $25,000.

What they want. None of these donors stated an ask in the sources read. The Katy News does not say how Lone Star Rising split spending between the primary against Jasmine Crockett and the general election.

Inference: The January gifts arrived before the March 3 primary. That suggests the PAC was built to win the nomination, which matters for reading who expects access. This is not shown in a filing.

## Opponent money: Musk and Senate Republican leadership

Documented. Musk gave SLF $75 million on September 9, 2026, the first day of the GOP midterm convention in Dallas. SLF put $75 million into Texas PAC on September 10. About two weeks later Musk gave $83 million more, and SLF moved $83 million to Texas PAC the next day, per the Texas Tribune and KERA on October 16, 2026, https://www.kera.org/2026/10/16/elon-musk-is-the-donor-behind-senate-republicans-massive-rescue-mission-for-ken-paxton/. AdImpact counted over $159 million in Texas PAC ads. Musk also seeded America PAC with nearly $90 million last quarter. America PAC put $18.8 million into the race through October 13, more than 80 percent against Talarico. MAGA Inc. had $33.7 million in pro-Paxton advertising by October 15.

What the attacks say. Texas PAC ads called Talarico "for they/them, not you." Republican groups used a clip of him saying "defending trans Texans is something we have to do every day," per the Texas Tribune on October 1, 2026, https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/01/texas-senate-gop-outside-spending-ken-paxton-thune-trump-talarico/. Allied GOP groups attacked his vote on a bill banning Texas land sales to China linked buyers, per the Texas Tribune on August 29, 2026. Paxton adviser Nick Maddux accused him of backing higher taxes, boys in girls' sports and defunding the police.

His response. His first negative ad, "Most Corrupt," ran August 29, 2026 as part of an eight figure buy. He calls Paxton "the most corrupt politician in Texas." He has also attacked Musk directly, per a CNBC headline of October 22, 2026 whose body was not read.

Inference: The Musk money is the story of the race. It also gives Talarico a populist frame he has used all fall.
