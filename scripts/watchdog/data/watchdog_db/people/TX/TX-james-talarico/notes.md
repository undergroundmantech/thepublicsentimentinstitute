## Campaign intel

- **Main message.** Ending billionaire control of politics and lowering costs, framed through faith and his record as a teacher; he calls Paxton "the most corrupt politician in Texas." https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/15/james-talarico-64-million-fundraising-texas-senate/ and https://www.texastribune.org/2026/08/29/james-talarico-ken-paxton-negative-television-ad-senate/
- **Biggest vulnerability as opponents frame it.** Culture war positions, led by his comments defending transgender Texans, packaged as "for they/them, not you," plus his vote on a China land sale ban. https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/01/texas-senate-gop-outside-spending-ken-paxton-thune-trump-talarico/
- **Strongest coalition.** In the TPOR poll fielded October 15 to 18, he led 51 to 47 overall, with independents 66 to 28, Black voters 87 to 11, Latino voters 60 to 39 and women 54 to 47; Paxton led white voters, men, and voters without a degree. https://www.fox4news.com/news/talarico-leads-paxton-new-poll-early-voting-continues-texas
- **Recent event.** Disclosure on October 15 and 16 that Musk was the money behind $158M routed through SLF to Texas PAC, which Talarico has turned into an anticorruption push aimed at both Paxton and Musk. https://www.texastribune.org/2026/10/15/elon-musk-ken-paxton-donations-senate-gop-super-pac/
- **Trend.** TPOR's lead for him narrowed from six points in August to four in October, with men shifting to Paxton since September. https://www.fox4news.com/news/talarico-leads-paxton-new-poll-early-voting-continues-texas
