# Vivek Ramaswamy research notes

Audit trail for OH-vivek-ramaswamy, compiled October 23, 2026. Search budget ran out partway through. Later work used direct fetches of known outlet pages and tag archives.

## Thread 1: Committees and totals

### Takeaway
There are two vehicles: the state candidate committee for the Ramaswamy and McColley ticket, and the federal super PAC V-PAC: Victors Not Victims, C00892919. Neither treasurer was found.

### Cited findings
- V-PAC FEC ID C00892919 and chief strategist Andy Surbian. V-PAC raised $16.8M in the first half of 2025 and held about $13M. https://signalohio.org/billionaire-makes-huge-contribution-to-political-group-backing-ohio-gop-candidate-vivek-ramaswamy/ July 30, 2025.
- V-PAC's first ad buy was $2.2M starting March 10, 2025. https://www.wvxu.org/politics/2025-04-04/analysis-vivek-ramaswamy-super-pac-trump April 4, 2025.
- The campaign raised $9.7M in the first half of 2025 and spent $2.5M. Strategist Jai Chabria spoke for it. https://www.journal-news.com/local/acton-far-behind-ramaswamy-in-governor-race-funding-despite-record-haul/ILYU33RADZD7ZPUE4NMXO5YW6I
- About $19.8M was raised by December 31, 2025, 57 percent from Ohio. https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/where-donations-came-ohio-governor-170000893.html
- Nearly $20M was raised since announcing. There were about 1,400 refunds totaling more than $230,000. https://www.news5cleveland.com/news/politics/ohio-politics/ohio-governors-race-set-to-become-most-expensive-in-state-history February 2, 2026.
- Cash was $12.9M at the start of 2026. V-PAC had $18.6M raised. https://signalcleveland.org/amy-actons-fundraising-surges-democratic-field-clears-in-2026-ohio-governors-race-election-2026 January 31, 2026.
- Pre-primary, he raised $5M from donors plus $25M of his own. Total raised since declaring, including his own money, was over $50M. https://www.nbc4i.com/news/your-local-election-hq/acton-ramaswamy-each-raise-over-5-million-from-donors-in-past-four-months/amp/ May 4, 2026.
- He raised $9.6M in 2026 by mid June and held $26.8M. https://signalohio.org/in-ohio-gubernatorial-race-amy-acton-outraises-vivek-ramaswamy-in-2026-death-penalty-divides-governor-and-attorney-general/ June 18, 2026.
- Monthly receipts were nearly $4M in July, about $2.6M in August and just over $3M in September. https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/10/13/ramaswamy-loans-campaign-5-million-more-as-acton-outpaces-him-with-donors/
- The aggregate shows $25,555,956 in contributions and $19,695,657 in expenditures, with no date range shown. https://www.transparencyusa.org/oh/candidate/vivek-ramaswamy

### Inferences
- The Transparency USA contribution figure probably excludes his loans, since it is far below the $50M+ figure that includes them. This is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- Committee ID and treasurer for both vehicles.
- Cash on hand after June 2026.
- V-PAC's third quarter federal report.

## Thread 2: Self funding and aircraft

### Takeaway
More than $36M of his own money has gone in, all as loans. A jet he owns through V Leasing LLC is leased to the campaign.

### Cited findings
- He lent $230,000 at the end of June 2025 for plane use. The plane is a 2011 Cessna Citation X, bought in September 2023. The V Leasing address matches his home. The campaign said donors do not pay for the flights. https://signalohio.org/vivek-ramaswamy-uses-his-private-jet-to-campaign-for-ohio-governor/
- The $25M loan was reported in the first quarter of 2026. About $120K went to V Leasing that quarter, and nearly $7M went to The Strategy Group. https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/04/27/ohio-republican-vivek-ramaswamy-holds-big-cash-lead-over-democrat-amy-acton-after-25-million-loan/
- He lent more than $5M in August and September 2026, with total own money committed above $36M. https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/10/13/ramaswamy-loans-campaign-5-million-more-as-acton-outpaces-him-with-donors/
- Aircraft spending was at least $555,676.15 from July to December 2025, including $239,905.32 to V Leasing. Other payees were Citation Management, Coshocton Air, Soin Aviation, Talon Air and TVPX. https://ramaswamyfiles.substack.com/p/private-aircraft-spending-in-vivek This is an Innovation Ohio advocacy source.
- V Leasing's payee total is $519,794.86. https://www.transparencyusa.org/oh/candidate/vivek-ramaswamy

### Inferences
- Because loans can be repaid from donor money, whether donors end up paying for the jet depends on post-election loan repayment.

### Gaps
- An itemized V Leasing payment schedule.
- Whether any loan repayments have been made.

V-PAC had raised $29.5M, with $23M cash at the end of March. https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/04/27/ohio-republican-vivek-ramaswamy-holds-big-cash-lead-over-democrat-amy-acton-after-25-million-loan/
- Second quarter: Musk $5M in May, Timashev $5M in May, Stevens $1M in May, Ackman $1M in June. Stevens gave $5M in early 2025. V-PAC spent over $8.1M in June against Acton and held $25.8M cash. Acton's response: "Ohio isn't for sale." https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/07/22/elon-musk-3-other-billionaires-put-12-million-behind-pro-ramaswamy-super-pac-in-ohio-governor-race/
- Second quarter itemized receipts were $12.24M. Raj Vattikuti gave $50K, Blockratize $10K and Michael Baker International $10K. https://ramaswamyfiles.substack.com/p/io-release-big-tech-is-bankrolling This is an Innovation Ohio release.
- Yass gave a further gift of at least $10M, reported by Politico, bringing him to at least $30M. Ramaswamy and allies had $82.3M in ads spent or reserved. https://signalohio.org/billionaire-jeff-yass-ohio-governor-race-november-election-2026/ August 27, 2026.
- V-PAC has spent $44.6M on advertising. https://www.newsfromthestates.com/article/outside-groups-pour-hundreds-millions-ohio-races-governor-us-senate October 20, 2026.

- Hop two: the Yass couple gave $110M to the GOP this cycle. Musk's cycle total is $369M, citing the Washington Post. Musk gave $158M to a super PAC that passed it to a group backing Paxton. https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/politics/2026/10/16/midterm-elections-top-50-donors-decision-2026

- Whether Yass funds Ohio school choice groups directly.

## Thread 5: Submetering and HB 173

### Takeaway
Submetering donors gave to the ticket and to legislative leaders around the June 2026 passage of HB 173, which DeWine vetoed. Ramaswamy has been silent.

### Cited findings
- HB 173 passed late at night on roughly party lines. Nationwide Energy Partners lobbyist Teresa Ringenbach testified in favor, and AES Ohio and Duke opposed. https://signalohio.org/ohio-supreme-court-set-safeguards-for-submeters-renters-electric-bills-lawmakers-rolled-them-back/ June 12, 2026.
- DeWine vetoed it on June 24, 2026, calling the model "fundamentally flawed." DeAscentis gave to Ramaswamy; the amount is garbled in the source. https://ohiocapitaljournal.com/2026/06/26/gov-dewine-vetoes-measure-ohio-submetering-legislation/
- The DeAscentis household gave $51,615 from June 5 to 8 to Ramaswamy, Huffman and Wilkin. Giller gave $20K in 2025. The industry total is over $58K. https://ramaswamyfiles.substack.com/p/vivek-ramaswamys-donors-got-a-law

### Inferences
- A refiled bill in 2027 would reach a governor whose running mate presided over the Senate that passed it.

### Gaps
- Whether an override is possible.
- Exact gift amounts from the SOS filing.
