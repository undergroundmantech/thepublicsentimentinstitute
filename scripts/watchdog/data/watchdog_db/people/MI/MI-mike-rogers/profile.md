# Mike Rogers Watchdog File

**Subject:** Mike Rogers, Republican nominee for U.S. Senate in Michigan, 2026, former U.S. Representative for MI-8 and 2024 Senate nominee, against Democrat Abdul El-Sayed
**Compiled:** October 25, 2026 for the On Point Politics watchdog database, onpointpolitics.us
**Data cutoff:** FEC data through September 30, 2026 as compiled by VoteLog, pre-general figures through October 14, 2026, independent expenditures through October 23, 2026.
**Next scheduled data drop:** FEC 48 hour reports through Election Day and the post-general report due December 3, 2026, which should show any UDP transfer to Great Lakes Conservatives Fund.

## Bottom line up front

Rogers lost to Elissa Slotkin in 2024 by just over 19,000 votes, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/. This time he trails El-Sayed in hard dollars but leads in cash and outside support. VoteLog shows $20,565,706 raised and $6,527,471 cash through September 30, 2026, and $132,512,494 in outside money benefiting him against $69,975,072 for El-Sayed, https://votelog.org/investigations/michigan-senate-general-money.

Interests with money and a live decision:

1. **Party and Trump world.** SLF at $40,657,615 and No Going Back PAC at $32,709,224 lead his outside side. SLF pledged more than $45 million in April.
2. **Pro-Israel.** NORPAC and RJC PAC gave directly, and Newsweek, relaying another outlet, says AIPAC's UDP is moving up to $9 million into Great Lakes Conservatives Fund after the October 14 disclosure deadline, https://www.newsweek.com/aipac-dumps-last-minute-9m-behind-mike-rogers-in-rebuke-of-abdul-el-sayed-12607752.
3. **Tech, finance and data centers.** He held board seats at AT&T, Nokia and Telefonica, sits on Forgepoint Capital's advisory council and holds a Blackstone REIT stake with heavy data center exposure. Seven Blackstone employees gave $34,500, https://planetdetroit.org/2026/09/data-centers-michigan-senate-rogers-el-sayed/. He backs a one year data center moratorium.
4. **Billionaire and corporate donors.** Ken Griffin maxed out; Timothy M. Dunn gave $5,000,000 to Great Lakes Conservatives Fund; the DeVos owned Alticor PAC gave.

## Committee structure and treasurer

The FEC candidate ID S4MI00595 appears in a search result for the FEC candidate page, which was not opened. The committee name, ID and treasurer were not verified. Spokesperson Alyssa Brouillet speaks for him. VoteLog shows $5,505,283 in transfers from authorized committees and 174 PACs giving $1,061,961.

## Party and Trump world

Documented. The NRSC gave $62,000 on August 27, 2025. No Going Back PAC is funded by MAGA Inc. at $200,000,000 across races. AFP Action counts $19,260,356 and America PAC $8,594,475 for him; America PAC's donors are not on the page. Rogers vowed to stand with Trump and repeated some false 2020 claims, per Bridge.

Inference: Outside groups supply most of his paid voice, as in 2024. His leverage with leadership is low and theirs with him is high.

## Pro-Israel

Documented. NORPAC gave $19,590 from September 14 to 30, 2026 and RJC PAC $11,121 across four dates, per VoteLog. Great Lakes Conservatives Fund raised $9.1 million in the third quarter, per Bridge, with Conservative Americans PAC at $10,140,000 and Timothy M. Dunn at $5,000,000 as top funders. Rogers said of AIPAC help, I'll take all of that help, https://www.newsweek.com/aipac-dumps-last-minute-9m-behind-mike-rogers-in-rebuke-of-abdul-el-sayed-12607752. He launched Democrats for Mike Rogers in Detroit on August 20, 2026. That group, renamed Together for Michigan, reported $50,000 from Great Lakes Conservatives Fund and $25,000 from American Unity PAC, https://www.latintimes.com/fec-records-expose-whos-actually-funding-democrats-mike-rogers-super-pac-it-isnt-democrats-600439.

Inference: A Democrats branded group funded by the main pro-Rogers super PAC is a disclosure flag worth watching.

## Tech, finance and data centers

Documented. PolitiFact reports board seats at AT&T, Nokia and Telefonica, over $1 million in corporate advising and consulting payments on his 2023 disclosure, and a $30,000 payment from lobbying firm CGCN Group before his 2024 run. It found he was never a registered lobbyist, https://api.politifact.com/factchecks/2026/jul/02/abdul-el-sayed/mike-rogers-14-million-pharma-lobbyist-michigan/. Planet Detroit reports his July 2026 disclosure lists $100,001 to $250,000 in Blackstone Real Estate Income Trust, which has 29 percent of its portfolio in data centers. It also relays The Lever's count of $1.7 million to $2.6 million in holdings that could benefit from tech and AI policy. Blackstone helps finance the $16 billion Oracle and OpenAI data center in Saline Township. He did not answer whether he would divest.

Inference: His moratorium stance runs against his own holdings, which is a point in his favor on independence, but his endorsement of Trump's AI permitting framework points the other way.

## Own finances and disclosure history

Per PolitiFact: non property assets of $415,600 to $605,600 in 2015; a 2025 disclosure listing $6.7 million to $13.5 million in assets with his wife and no debt; an estimated total of $10.1 million to $16.9 million with property; homes in Michigan, Virginia and Florida assessed at about $3.2 million in total.

## Enforcement and controversies

- **Pharma lobbyist claim.** El-Sayed's claim of a $14 million pharma lobbyist payout was rated Pants on Fire. Rogers sent a cease and desist on June 22, 2026, https://api.politifact.com/factchecks/2026/jul/02/abdul-el-sayed/mike-rogers-14-million-pharma-lobbyist-michigan/.
- **2020 and 2024 election claims.** Bridge reports he repeated some false 2020 claims and questioned his own 2024 loss, which Slotkin disputed, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/.
- **Together for Michigan.** The Rogers campaign did not respond about a $30,000 payment the group made to Melanie Rutherford, https://www.latintimes.com/fec-records-expose-whos-actually-funding-democrats-mike-rogers-super-pac-it-isnt-democrats-600439.
- No FEC or ethics matter was found in sources read.
