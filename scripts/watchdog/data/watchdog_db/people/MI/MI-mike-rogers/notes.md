- **Biggest vulnerability as opponents frame it.** El-Sayed says he is in bed with big pharma, wealthy from trading on congressional relationships, and has a war for everything; the pharma lobbyist version of that attack was rated Pants on Fire. https://www.clickondetroit.com/news/local/2026/10/22/rogers-el-sayed-battle-over-immigration-health-care-and-iran-in-senate-debate/ and https://api.politifact.com/factchecks/2026/jul/02/abdul-el-sayed/mike-rogers-14-million-pharma-lobbyist-michigan/

- **Crossover play.** He launched Democrats for Mike Rogers in Detroit on August 20 to court Democrats uneasy with El-Sayed, including Jewish Democrats. https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/
- **Recent event.** Reports that AIPAC's super PAC is moving up to $9 million to a pro-Rogers group after the October 14 deadline. https://www.newsweek.com/aipac-dumps-last-minute-9m-behind-mike-rogers-in-rebuke-of-abdul-el-sayed-12607752
- **Track record.** He ran about 123,000 votes behind Trump in Michigan in 2024. https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/
