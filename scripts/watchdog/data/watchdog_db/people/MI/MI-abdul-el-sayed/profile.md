# Abdul El-Sayed Watchdog File

**Subject:** Abdul El-Sayed, Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate in Michigan, 2026, for the open seat Gary Peters is leaving, against Republican Mike Rogers
**Compiled:** October 25, 2026 for the On Point Politics watchdog database, onpointpolitics.us
**Data cutoff:** FEC data through September 30, 2026 as compiled by VoteLog, pre-general figures through October 14, 2026, independent expenditures through October 23, 2026.
**Next scheduled data drop:** FEC 48 hour reports through Election Day and the post-general report due December 3, 2026, which should show late transfers on both sides.

## Bottom line up front

El-Sayed beat Rep. Haley Stevens by less than a point in the August 4 primary despite heavy AIPAC aligned spending against him. He then set a Michigan record with $30.7 million raised from July 16 to September 30, 2026, per Bridge Michigan, https://bridgemi.com/uncategorized/abdul-el-sayed-sets-michigan-fundraising-record-see-whos-contributing/. VoteLog shows $45,175,567 raised through September 30, $39,666,902 of it from individuals and only $364,721 from PACs, https://votelog.org/investigations/michigan-senate-general-money. He spends nearly all of it; cash was $3.8 million at September 30 against Rogers' $6.5 million.

Interests with money and a live decision:

1. **Pro-Israel groups, opposing.** UDP, AIPAC's super PAC, spent about $33 million in the primary against him and for Stevens, per AdImpact via Newsweek, and nearly $100,000 on general election mailers, https://www.newsweek.com/aipac-dumps-last-minute-9m-behind-mike-rogers-in-rebuke-of-abdul-el-sayed-12607752. Newsweek, relaying another outlet, says UDP is moving up to $9 million to a pro-Rogers super PAC. The lever is U.S. aid to Israel, which he criticizes.
2. **SMP and party.** WinSenate at $36,579,898 is the largest group on his side. The DSCC gave $62,000 on August 5, 2026.
3. **Progressive movement.** Bernie Sanders endorsed him and is reported to have sent $1 million; Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez sent $1 million to a joint fundraising account. Both are Secondary until the post-general report.
4. **Labor.** Fighting for Michigan PAC counts $3,081,423 for him, and VoteLog lists a UAW related entry of $900,000 as its top funder.

## Committee structure and treasurer

The committee name, FEC ID and treasurer were not verified. VoteLog shows $4,953,348 in transfers from authorized committees, consistent with the joint fundraising account that received the Ocasio-Cortez transfer. PAC donors are few: 49 PACs gave $277,196, led by the DSCC, A New Policy PAC at $25,703 and Progressive Voters of America at $10,000.

## Small donors

Documented. About $11.2 million of his cycle total came from donors under $200, over a third of the total. About 55,000 donors who gave under $200 in the quarter but more than $200 for the cycle gave $2.5 million. About 28 percent of itemized money came from Michigan. Named max donors include Microsoft CTO Kevin Scott and several actors, https://bridgemi.com/uncategorized/abdul-el-sayed-sets-michigan-fundraising-record-see-whos-contributing/. He raised $8.7 million more from October 1 to 14, https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/oct/23/senate-democratic-candidates-keep-the-cash-flowing/.

Inference: His money is national and small. That insulates him from any one donor but invites the out of state attack.

## Pro-Israel

Documented. His message on money is money out of politics, money in your pocket, Medicare for All, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/. He criticizes U.S. aid to Israel and AIPAC's spending. The Michigan Jewish Democratic Caucus said his AIPAC criticism functioned as a dog whistle. Rogers has said of AIPAC help, I'll take all of that help. El-Sayed called Rogers an AIPAC backed politician at a debate. AIPAC spokesperson Deryn Sousa said members were highly motivated, https://www.newsweek.com/aipac-dumps-last-minute-9m-behind-mike-rogers-in-rebuke-of-abdul-el-sayed-12607752.

Inference: This is the bloc with the most money aimed at the race and the clearest stated ask. The late transfer timing means the public record will lag Election Day.

## Party and leadership

Documented. WinSenate is funded by SMP at $171,120,000 across races. Lower Costs Now and Defend The Vote add $6,244,837 and $4,592,976. The state party and Stevens endorsed him after the primary.

## Labor unions

Documented. Fighting for Michigan PAC spent about $1.5 million in the third quarter per Bridge. VoteLog lists a UAW related entry of $900,000 and Allen Liu at $355,700 as its funders. Bridge notes some unions worry about losing employer plans under single payer, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/.

## Own finances and disclosure history

He holds $50,001 to $100,000 in the Amana Growth Fund, which holds Apple, Alphabet and Nvidia. His spokesperson says he owns no stock directly, https://planetdetroit.org/2026/09/data-centers-michigan-senate-rogers-el-sayed/. No net worth estimate was found.

## Enforcement and controversies

- **Pharma lobbyist claim.** PolitiFact rated Pants on Fire his June 14, 2026 claim that Rogers took a $14 million payout as a pharma lobbyist. Rogers sent a cease and desist on June 22. El-Sayed called it a mistake in a later video while saying Rogers spent years helping the pharmaceutical industry, https://api.politifact.com/factchecks/2026/jul/02/abdul-el-sayed/mike-rogers-14-million-pharma-lobbyist-michigan/.
- **Muslim Brotherhood allegation.** A Hill headline reports Republican attacks alleging ties; the body did not load, so the allegation and his response are not stored.
- **Hasan Piker.** Jewish Democrats criticized his association with the streamer, https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/mike-rogers-vs-abdul-el-sayed-7-issues-defining-michigans-us-senate-race/. His response was not found.
