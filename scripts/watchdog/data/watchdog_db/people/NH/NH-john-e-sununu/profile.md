# John E. Sununu Watchdog File

**Subject:** John E. Sununu, Republican nominee for U.S. Senate in New Hampshire, 2026, U.S. Senator from 2003 to 2009 and a former three term congressman
**Compiled:** October 25, 2026 for the On Point Politics watchdog database, onpointpolitics.us
**Data cutoff:** FEC data through October 14, 2026 and independent expenditures through October 23, 2026, as compiled by VoteLog on October 24.
**Next scheduled data drop:** Post general FEC report due December 3, 2026, and 24 hour independent expenditure reports through Election Day.

## Bottom line up front

Sununu's own campaign is small. Outside money is carrying him. VoteLog shows his campaign at $5,681,528 raised, $3,936,991 spent and $1,711,396 in cash through October 14, 2026, with no self funding, https://votelog.org/investigations/new-hampshire-senate-general-money. Outside groups had spent $36,057,061 benefiting him in the general election, against $19,279,169 for Pappas.

Three interests combine money and a decision:

1. **Billionaire megadonors.** The Senate Leadership Fund, funded by Elon Musk at $168 million cycle wide, Miriam Adelson at $30 million and others, spent $11,118,531 for him. Americans for Prosperity Action, America PAC and Protect Freedom PAC add Koch, Musk and Jeff Yass money.
2. **Trump world.** No Going Back PAC, backed by MAGA Inc.'s $200 million cycle wide, spent $14,037,292 for him. Trump endorsed him on February 1, 2026.
3. **Wall Street and private equity.** Blackstone executives gave $48,000 and Apollo CEO Marc Rowan and his wife $24,000, per American Journal News, https://americanjournalnews.com/sununus-campaign-fueled-by-private-equity-cash/. He worked at Akin Gump, whose clients included both firms. Blackstone CEO Stephen Schwarzman gave SLF $20 million cycle wide.

## Committee structure and treasurer

The campaign committee name, FEC ID and treasurer were not verified; the roster has no FEC ID for him. Main outside vehicles: No Going Back PAC Inc., FEC C00960435, formed September 1, 2026, which shares a treasurer, address and phone number with MAGA Inc., per Granite Post, https://granitepostnews.com/news/politics/trump-super-pac-sununu/; and the Senate Leadership Fund, FEC C00571703.

## Billionaire megadonors

Documented, per VoteLog. SLF spent $11,118,531 for him in the general election. Its cycle wide donors include Elon Musk at $168,000,000, One Nation at $132,907,000, Miriam Adelson at $30,000,000, Stephen Schwarzman at $20,000,000 and Paul Singer at $14,500,000. AFP Action spent $7,389,773; Koch Inc. and Stand Together gave it $66 million combined. America PAC, funded by Musk at $20.1 million, spent $865,563. Protect Freedom PAC, funded by Jeff Yass at $9.5 million, spent $864,273. Donor totals are cycle wide across all races and are not attributable to New Hampshire. TIME reported SLF at $17 million for Sununu as of October 5, likely including primary spending, https://time.com/article/2026/10/05/new-hampshire-blue-wave-republicans-sununu-pappas/.

Inference: These donors want a Republican Senate majority more than any New Hampshire specific decision. The lever is control of the chamber and the confirmation, tax and regulatory agenda that follows.

## Trump world

Documented. No Going Back PAC booked $9.6 million in New Hampshire ads the day Sununu won the primary and spent $14,037,292 by October 23. A No Going Back mailer says Pappas sold out to big insurance, per NHPR, https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-09-28/nh-newhampshire-senate-race-pappas-sununu-midterms-election. Trump endorsed Sununu on February 1, 2026, and Sununu said the race is important to Trump's agenda and that he would absolutely campaign with him.

Inference: Sununu ran against Trump aligned candidates in past presidential primaries, backing Kasich in 2016 and Haley in 2024 per the Union Leader, yet his largest single outside backer is now a MAGA Inc. vehicle.

## Wall Street and private equity

Documented. After losing his seat in 2008 he worked at Akin Gump, whose clients included Blackstone and Apollo, and joined TAP Advisors in 2014, per American Journal News. That outlet is progressive aligned and its figures are stored as Secondary. A WinSenate release says Akin Gump paid him a generous salary and that his stock portfolio is worth up to $2 million, without figures or traceable sources, https://senatemajority.com/news/winsenate-hits-john-e-sununu-for-extensive-history-of-profiting-from-higher-prices-in-new-hampshire/.

Inference: Private equity tax treatment is the live lever for this bloc. No stated position was found.

## Own finances and disclosure history

Sununu says much of the $35 million on his financial disclosure reflects the value of the Waterville Valley ski resort and adjacent land his family owns, per the Union Leader as syndicated on Yahoo, https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/sununu-pappas-clash-over-taxes-035900891.html. The disclosure itself was not read.

## Enforcement and controversies

- **Cashing in.** Contested. Pappas ads say Sununu sold out to Big Pharma and pocketed $35 million since 2008. Sununu calls it a lie, says he never worked for Big Pharma and never made $35 million, and says Pappas confuses income and assets.
- **Tax attack.** Sununu said Pappas voted to raise taxes 68 times; PolitiFact rated that False on October 1, 2026.
- **Forum cancellation.** NHPR and the Granite State News Collaborative cancelled a Senate forum after a Sununu spokesman said he could not attend given the schedule, per NHPR, https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-10-21/nh-newhampshire-senate-race-pappas-sununu-2026-election.
- No FEC or ethics enforcement matter was found in sources read.
