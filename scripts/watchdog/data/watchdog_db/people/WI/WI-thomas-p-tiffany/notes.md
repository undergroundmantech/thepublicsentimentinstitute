# Notes: Tom Tiffany, WI Governor 2026

Status key used in edges.csv: Confirmed means a primary filing, or an article body read where the figure is under $100,000 or two independent outlets agree. Secondary means a single outlet, a figure read only through another site, or a campaign claim.

## Thread 1: Committees and totals

**Takeaway.** The state committee and the federal committee share the name Tiffany for Wisconsin. The state committee is WEC 0104212 with treasurer James Koth. The federal committee is FEC C00718635 with treasurer Fred Koth, per ProPublica.

**Cited findings.**
- September 2026 report, July 1 to Sept 23, 2026: $26,314,950.42 raised; $24,260,750 in transfers; $2,053,700.42 from 9,554 individual transactions. Year to date: $39,653,484.86 raised and $34,708,000 in transfers in. Disbursements were $18,935,600.15, and ending cash was $12,349,450.19. Source: https://campaignfinance.wi.gov/report-download/0104212-CF-2--2026_September_Report--ORIGINAL--cover--2026-09-30-22-09-04.pdf
- Jan to June 2026: $8.7M raised, $7.4M spent, nearly $3M cash, and over $6.1M in RPW transfers, per https://wisconsinexaminer.com/2026/07/16/tiffany-reports-over-8-million-haul-for-gov-race-while-barnes-leads-dem-field-in-fundraising/ and https://www.wbay.com/2026/07/16/wisconsin-governors-race-finance-reports-show-wide-cash-on-hand-gaps/
- July 1 to 27, 2026: $4.6M raised, just over $4M of it from RPW, and nearly $600K from individuals. RPW transfers to date were about $10.4M. Sources: https://www.wbay.com/2026/08/04/wisconsin-governors-race-new-campaign-finance-reports-show-crowley-leads-democratic-field-fundraising/ and https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=989724
- July to Dec 2025: $2,122,489 raised, $438,160 spent, and $1,695,038 cash, per https://wisconsinexaminer.com/2026/01/16/crowley-and-tiffany-lead-fundraising-in-governors-race/
- Federal 2024 cycle: $1,259,827 in receipts and $1,460,799 in disbursements, per https://projects.propublica.org/itemizer/committee/C00718635/2024
- The campaign was budgeting $40M as of Dec 29, 2025, per https://www.wpr.org/news/republican-primary-governor-candidates-trump-fundraising

**Inferences.** The ending cash of $12.3M on Sept 23 means the campaign entered October with about four times Crowley's cash.

**Gaps.** Vendor lists for the state committee are missing. Full report PDFs and the browse pages refused access.

## Thread 2: The party conduit and the billionaires

**Takeaway.** Hendricks and the Uihleins funded RPW, and RPW transferred money to Tiffany on matching dates. Each leg is stored separately.

**Cited findings.**

  - Richard Uihlein: $5M on 8/10 and $6M on 9/15
  - Elizabeth Uihlein: $5M on 8/11 and $6M on 9/16
  - Hendricks: $5M, $500K and $100K, all on 8/31
  - Kunes: $200K on 8/31
  - Neumann: $150K on 9/14
  - Simon: $100K on 8/28
  - RPW totals for the period: $29,586,524.64 in contributions and $25,219,059.71 in transfers out.
- Tiffany's transfers in schedule shows eleven RPW transfers totaling $24.2M between 8/13 and 9/19, per https://campaignfinance.wi.gov/report-download/0104212-CF-2--2026_September_Report--ORIGINAL--Transfers_in_table--2026-09-30-22-09-04.pdf
- 2026 donor totals per WisPolitics, Oct 2:
  - Hendricks: $19.9M total, including $8.1M to RPW, $8M to RAC and $3.75M to CERS
  - Richard Uihlein: $28.5M total, including $15.5M to RPW and $10M to Restoration PAC in August
  - Elizabeth Uihlein: $24.7M total, including $15.6M to RPW, $7M to RAC and $2M to CERS
  - Restoration spent $9.7M in the September period, mostly against Crowley.
  - Source: https://www.wispolitics.com/2026/hendricks-the-uihleins-gave-more-than-73-million-to-gop-committees-candidates-since-jan-1/
- AP, Oct 1: RPW got about $38M from the three, about $34M of Tiffany's roughly $43M came by party transfer, Restoration has spent about $12.5M, and AdImpact shows $40M to $26M in ads. Source: https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/billionaires-give-tiffany-a-big-cash-boost-in-the-wisconsins-2026-race-for-governor/
- WPR, Oct 1: the Uihleins gave $22M and Hendricks $5.6M. Tiffany called the 2015 law a billionaire loophole despite voting for it. Source: https://www.wpr.org/news/funds-from-3-billionaires-give-tom-tiffany-huge-fundraising-lead-race-governor
- Jan to June 2026: the Uihleins gave RPW $4M and Hendricks $2.5M, per the Examiner, July 16.
- Wisconsin Watch fact brief: $42M raised since July 2025, about 80 percent of it from the party, per https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/wisconsin-tom-tiffany-campaign-donations-billionaires-hendricks-uihlein-governor-election-republican-party/

**Inferences.** The gift and transfer dates match closely. The filings show no earmark. Treat the party as a conduit in effect, but do not store a donor to Tiffany edge.

**Gaps.** No source read documents a specific policy ask by Hendricks or the Uihleins, or any state contracts or tax credits held by ABC Supply or Uline.

## Thread 3: Utilities, data centers and energy

**Takeaway.** Utility money to Tiffany is small. The lever is the PSC and the data center exemption.

**Cited findings.**
- WEC PAC gave $2,000 at the state level from 2010 to 2019 and $9,500 at the federal level from 2019 to 2023, with nothing since the governor run began, per https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/04/wisconsin-tom-tiffany-we-energies-pac-donation-election-governor-campaign-finance/
- About $3,600 came through WEC and MGE conduits in 2026, with no utility PAC money from Jan to Sept. Tiffany said the acting commissioners will be replaced day one. Source: https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/campaign-contributions-from-wisconsin-utilities-are-down-for-the-2026-election/ and the original at https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/wisconsin-utilities-campaign-finance-spending-energy-future-data-centers-high-stakes/
- The PSC has three commissioners serving six year terms, appointed by the governor and confirmed by the Senate. Pending and recent cases include the We Energies Very Large Customer tariff and an over $2B gas plan approved May 2025, with 40 percent demand growth projected from 2026 to 2030. Source: https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/02/wisconsin-public-service-commission-data-center-boom-utilities-power-energy/
- The exemption costs $1.5B on construction from 2024 to 2028 and $369M a year on operations, per the LFB via the Wisconsin Policy Forum: https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/09/wisconsin-data-center-incentives-taxes-report-energy-water-usage-electricity/
- Tiffany called data centers exciting new technology in Jan 2026 and voted for the Dec 2025 permitting bill, Roll Call 356, per https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/wisconsin-crowley-tiffany-data-centers-support-artificial-intelligence-governor-election/
- At the debate he made the repeal pledge and set out positions on the surplus, vouchers and transportation, per https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/david-crowley-tom-tiffany-clash-on-taxes-abortion-and-private-school-vouchers-in-wisconsin-governor-debate/
- Filed PAC transfers: KOCHPAC $15K on 8/4 and Peabody PAC $5K on 8/11. The Peabody line carries a note to verify its registration.

**Inferences.** Tiffany's repeal pledge puts him on the side of ratepayers against tech, at little donor cost.

**Gaps.** No Roll Call 356 bill number was captured. No Line 5, mining or wolf votes were captured; the House press page only showed the Sept 4, 2026 wolf delisting release.
