# Notes: David Crowley, WI Governor 2026

Status key used in edges.csv: Confirmed means a primary filing, or an article body read where the figure is under $100,000 or two independent outlets agree. Secondary means a single outlet, a figure read only through another site, or a campaign claim.

## Thread 1: Committees and totals

**Takeaway.** Crowley for Wisconsin is WEC 0105751, with treasurer Nick Truog. Its September report shows $10.2M raised and $2.83M in ending cash.

**Cited findings.**
- Sept 2026 report, July 1 to Sept 23: $10,203,455.52 raised; $6,054,458.19 from individuals; $4,138,600.01 in transfers; $7,836,643.85 in disbursements; $2,829,882.15 ending cash. Year to date: $11,229,316.58. Source: https://campaignfinance.wi.gov/report-download/0105751-CF-2--2026_September_Report--ORIGINAL--cover--2026-10-01-04-10-96.pdf
- The transfers schedule lists 62 rows. The itemized rows sum to $4,138,601.01, which is $1 above the printed total. Source: https://campaignfinance.wi.gov/report-download/0105751-CF-2--2026_September_Report--ORIGINAL--Transfers_in_table--2026-10-01-04-10-96.pdf
- July 1 to 27, 2026: about $776K raised, $628K spent and $463K cash, including $20K from the Evers campaign, per https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=989724
- July to Dec 2025: $800,949 raised, $187,529 spent and $602,181 cash. About $138K was transferred from his county committee. Seven donors gave $20K; Alex Lasry was the only one named. Source: https://wisconsinexaminer.com/2026/01/16/crowley-and-tiffany-lead-fundraising-in-governors-race/
- The Examiner reported $10.2M raised, $7.8M spent, $2.8M cash and $2.7M from DPW, per https://wisconsinexaminer.com/?p=48111
- Campaign claims, unverified:
  - $550K raised in five days, per a July 24 release
  - $775K raised since reentry, per https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=987831
  - $1M raised in the 24 hours after the primary, per WPR

**Inferences.** WPR's figure of about $4M from the party, unions and PACs matches the total transfers figure, not party money alone. The filing shows $2.73M came from DPW.

**Gaps.** The Jan to June 2026 report was not read. No vendors were captured.

## Thread 2: Labor

**Takeaway.** Building trades and public sector unions are the core institutional donors. Many gave the $86,000 cap.

**Cited findings.**
- As of 8/13, per https://dailyreporter.com/?p=554501: Laborers District Council $86K, IBEW PAC $86K, Pipe Trades $86K, Operating Engineers 139 $84K, Carpenters $45K, Steamfitters 601 $25K. Endorsements came from the Laborers, IBEW, the Carpenters regional council, Plumbers 75 and the AFL-CIO.
- The Sept filing lists the following, per the schedule above:
  - LIUNA PAC WI: $86K on 8/27
  - WEAC PAC: $86K on 8/31
  - AFSCME People: $86K on 9/16
  - Local 158 PAC, Active Ballot Club and DRIVE Committee: $86K each on 9/18
  - Plumbers 75: $10K on 7/29 and $76K on 9/1
  - Carpenters: $20K, $25K and $37.5K
  - Local 400 COPE: $25K twice
  - smaller checks from other unions
- IBEW supports responsible data center development, per https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/wisconsin-utilities-campaign-finance-spending-energy-future-data-centers-high-stakes/
- Positions on the data center repeal, vouchers, tolls and prisons are covered at https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/david-crowley-tom-tiffany-clash-on-taxes-abortion-and-private-school-vouchers-in-wisconsin-governor-debate/
- The data hub quote is at https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/10/wisconsin-crowley-tiffany-data-centers-support-artificial-intelligence-governor-election/

**Inferences.** The repeal pledge diverges from the building trades' interest. The voucher stance diverges from the teachers unions' interest. Neither union position was sourced directly in this pass, so both inferences remain labeled.

**Gaps.** The Laborers District Council and LIUNA PAC WI may be related committees. The parent unions of Local 158, Active Ballot Club and DRIVE were not resolved. The Act 10 position was not sourced.

## Thread 3: Party and billionaire donors upstream

**Takeaway.** DPW moved $2.73M to Crowley. DPW's large donors include Pritzker, Lynde Uihlein and Hoffman.

**Cited findings.**
- DPW checks: $170K and $250K on 8/12, $580K on 8/13, $500K on 8/31, $1M on 9/9, and $230K on 9/23. Source: the filing.
- AP: Pritzker and Lynde Uihlein gave $1M each in the past two months, and Hoffman about $15.5M since 2019. Source: https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/billionaires-give-tiffany-a-big-cash-boost-in-the-wisconsins-2026-race-for-governor/
- WPR: DPW raised nearly $6M, including $565K from the Evers fund, and is down 49 percent from 2022. Source: https://www.wpr.org/news/funds-from-3-billionaires-give-tom-tiffany-huge-fundraising-lead-race-governor

**Inferences.** The party conduit is the same structure as Tiffany's, at about a tenth of the scale.

**Gaps.** The DPW filing was not read. The dates of the Pritzker and Lynde Uihlein gifts are not known.

## Thread 4: Outside money and controversies

**Takeaway.** The outside money against Crowley is roughly twice the outside money for him in the sources read.

**Cited findings.**
- Restoration of America spent $9.7M, per WisPolitics. First Amendment LLC spent $9.3M and Right Direction Wisconsin ran ads, both per Ballotpedia: https://ballotpedia.org/Wisconsin_gubernatorial_election,_2026
- The DGA's seven figure buy and Tiffany's ad claims are reported at https://www.wpr.org/news/wisconsins-general-election-ad-war-governor
- The RGA spent $3.6M in the primary, a campaign claim: https://urbanmilwaukee.com/pressrelease/crowley-republican-governors-association-doubles-down-on-its-preferred-candidate-with-another-six-figure-ad-buy-bringing-total-wisconsin-spending-to-3-6-million/
- The withdrawal and the July 18 reentry are reported at https://www.wpr.org/news/david-crowley-returns-governors-race-tony-evers-democratic-primary-2026 and https://urbanmilwaukee.com/?p=984093
- Utility money is reported at https://pbswisconsin.org/news-item/campaign-contributions-from-wisconsin-utilities-are-down-for-the-2026-election/

**Inferences.** None beyond the profile.

**Gaps.** No source was found for the county health insurance or sales tax record behind Tiffany's ad. No Statement of Economic Interests was found. No enforcement matters were found.
