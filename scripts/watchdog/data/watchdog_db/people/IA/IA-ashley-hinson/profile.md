# Ashley Hinson Watchdog File

**Subject:** Ashley Hinson, Republican nominee for U.S. Senate in Iowa, 2026, U.S. Representative for IA-2
**Compiled:** October 25, 2026 for the On Point Politics watchdog database, onpointpolitics.us
**Data cutoff:** October 24, 2026. The latest candidate figures found are second quarter 2026 FEC reports through June 30, 2026.
**Next scheduled data drop:** FEC pre-general report covering through October 14, 2026, not found in this pass, then the post-general report due December 3, 2026.

## Bottom line up front

Hinson is the party's candidate with the cash. She raised $10,262,866 through June 30, 2026 and held $6,749,491, nearly three times Josh Turek's cash, per Little Village on July 17, 2026, https://littlevillagemag.com/turek-democrats-outraise-republicans/. The NRSC committed $29 million to help her and One Nation planned $11 million.

Four interests combine money with a decision she would touch:

1. **Senate Republican leadership.** The NRSC, the Senate Leadership Fund and One Nation carry her air war. The decision is the Senate majority in January 2027.
2. **Agribusiness and the carbon pipeline.** Bruce Rastetter, founder of Summit Agricultural Group, gives and raises for her. Summit's pipeline is the most unpopular project in Iowa farm politics, and she has no public position on it in sources read.
3. **Her own holdings.** Democrats say her household kept $1 million to $5 million of private insurance agency stock while she backs a narrower stock bill. Her campaign calls the attack a lie. The decision is a congressional ownership ban.
4. **Health care.** She voted for the 2025 law that Democrats say cut Medicaid, which is the center of more than $30 million in Senate Majority PAC reservations against her.

## Senate Republican leadership

Documented. The NRSC committed $29 million, per Scripps on June 2, 2026, https://www.abc15.com/politics/paralympic-athlete-wins-key-iowa-democratic-senate-primary. One Nation, a sister group of the Senate Leadership Fund, planned $11 million in ads on the estate tax, veterans and costs. The Senate Leadership Fund flew an anti-Turek banner at the State Fair, per Bleeding Heartland on August 24, 2026, https://www.bleedingheartland.com/?p=458547. Trump and Joni Ernst endorsed her, Ernst on January 10, 2026, per Ballotpedia News, https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/08/06/turek-hinson-running-for-open-u-s-senate-seat-in-iowa-as-ernst-retires/.

Inference: Leadership groups, not individual donors, are her largest source of support. Their ask is a reliable Senate vote.

## Agribusiness and the carbon pipeline

Documented. Bleeding Heartland reports that Rastetter, a figure behind the Summit Carbon Solutions pipeline, has donated to Hinson and is helping her raise from other large donors. An Iowa Democratic Party release of August 11, 2026 relays Iowa Starting Line: Rastetter and Summit CEO Justin Kirchhoff hosted an August 5 fundraiser for her and together have given nearly $60,000 to her committees since 2019, https://iowademocrats.org/?p=38664. That figure is Secondary because the underlying report was not read. The same release cites a March 2023 Iowa Poll finding 78 percent of Iowans opposed eminent domain for pipelines.

What they want. A path for the pipeline and federal carbon capture support.

Inference: This is the clearest case in the file of a bloc with money and no stated position. Her primary rival Jim Carlin used it against her and still took just under 26 percent.

## Own finances and disclosure history

A Heartland Signal report relayed by the Iowa Democratic Party on June 12, 2026 says her household net worth rose from at most $1.6 million before office to as much as $8 million by 2024. It says her husband sold his stake in the Elliott Hartman Agency to a private equity platform for more than $1 million within months of her taking office, and the household kept $1 million to $5 million in private stock, https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/breaking-iowa-senate-candidate-ashley-hinson-touts-congressional-stock-ban-while-a-loophole-fueled-her-own-wealth. A second release on July 6, 2026 relays MeidasTouch, https://iowademocrats.org/2026/07/06/breaking-ashley-hinson-lied-about-owning-stock-while-campaigning-against-insider-trading-in-congress/. Her campaign told KCAU on June 16 that this was a shameless lie from partisan hacks. It said the couple sold individual stocks and hold ETFs and mutual funds, while her husband retains private stock in the business. Status Contested. Her own disclosure was not read.

## Enforcement and controversies

- No FEC or House Ethics matter against Hinson was found in sources read. She sits on the House Ethics Committee per the roster.
- Senate Majority PAC ads on August 24 say she missed nearly 200 votes and backed a tax break for her family business. Her response to those specific claims was not found.
- Turek's campaign says she skipped part of a June 24 defense markup for a fundraiser, per Fox News, https://www.foxnews.com/politics/dem-senate-hopeful-pocketed-massive-taxpayer-funded-cash-while-skipping-hundreds-votes.
