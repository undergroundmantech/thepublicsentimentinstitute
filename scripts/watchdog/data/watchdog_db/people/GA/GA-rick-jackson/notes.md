## Thread 1: Committees and IDs

### Takeaway
Two vehicles: the candidate committee and For All Georgians Inc., a leadership committee opened in June 2026 after the runoff win. Treasurer and filer IDs are not found.

### Cited findings
- For All Georgians Inc. named as his leadership committee, created under the 2021 law; RGA gave it $10 million. Georgia Recorder via Cobb Courier, Aug 12 2026: https://cobbcountycourier.com/2026/08/gop-candidates-outraise-democrats-so-far-in-most-but-not-all-of-georgias-statewide-contests/

- For All Georgians Inc. gave the campaign $5 million; campaign raised $6.1M in the Jun 11 to Jul 31 period; $4.3M cash end of July; more than $118M raised or given overall; corporate gifts over $240,000; nearly $200,000 from outside Georgia. Capitol Beat Aug 11 2026: https://roughdraftatlanta.com/2026/08/11/rick-jackson-outpaces-bottoms-fundraising/ ; also republished at https://www.mainstreetnews.com/jackson/state_news/jackson-leads-bottoms-in-fundraising-for-georgia-governors-race/article_fa62134f-505d-4354-86ce-09398e50fabd.html , which is the same reporting and not an independent source.

AJC Feb 2026: https://www.ajc.com/politics/2026/02/how-georgias-unlimited-money-machine-is-powering-the-governors-race/ and GPB Feb 24 2026: https://www.gpb.org/news/2026/02/24/judge-rules-against-burt-jones-unlimited-fundraising-in-race-for-georgia-governor
